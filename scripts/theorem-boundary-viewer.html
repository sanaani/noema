Reading the axes
The gray cross marks (0, 0), the projected origin. Tick labels show coordinate units. The original vectors have length 1; the projection shows only two components, so its points generally lie less than 1 unit from zero.
Shared axes give every theorem the same coordinate frame. Local axes are fitted separately for each theorem; their rotations and positions cannot be compared. Zero is a coordinate reference and is never added to the boundary.
What overlap means here
The comparison overlays both theorems in the same projection. Overlapping pictures do not establish intersection in the original space. A single closed polygon also imposes connectivity within each drawn boundary.
