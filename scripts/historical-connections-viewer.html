Historical connections: are the theorem centers close?

Four examples, selected before measuring. Each point is the embedding of a proved theorem’s initial goal. Distances use all 1,472 dimensions. This pilot tests centers; it does not measure cumulative proof-State mass or the full Theorem Density Objects.

Comparison across examples

“Background farther away” is the fraction of 64 background theorems farther from an anchor than its historical partner is. Higher means the partner is relatively close. The two directions can differ because the anchors have different surroundings. Background theorems are not certified unrelated.
