# Fresh, structurally matched strategy-transfer experiment

The user's instruction to keep going authorizes a new design after v1's closed
candidate. Preserve all v1 outcomes and its prohibition on scoring its clouds.
This protocol is a local Git preregistration before any v2 embeddings or scores.
It does not open Phase 5 or mathlib acquisition.

## Design change and eligibility

Keep v1's independently replayed transfer relation, five-step shortest rotation
programs, minimum eight programs per theorem, and positive mutual transfer >=.75
versus negative transfer zero. Use eight leaves for development, nine leaves
for conditional confirmation: neither split reuses the seven-leaf v1 task.

Within every anchor/positive/negative triplet, require EXACT equality of the
source-clade intersection counts AND target-clade intersection counts. Equal
leaf counts then imply equal source and target Jaccard distances separately,
not merely their averaged score. This selection uses symbolic syntax and the
transfer oracle, never learned embeddings, cloud distances, or previous scores.
Keep equal token-bag distances and used-premise overlap, crossed 0/1 as in v1.
Randomize operation and variable names independently of the relationship label.

No theorem endpoint pair or shortest proof program may recur across triplets.
Within a triplet, positive program sharing defines the intended relationship;
the negative program bank must be disjoint from BOTH positive members' banks.
This stronger exclusion removes direct strategy reuse across sampling units.
It does not establish independence of every latent structural feature; report
the finite-population and conditional-sampling limits explicitly.

A geometry-free feasibility script (seed 914202610) found 64 such disjoint
triplets in the 23,148-record eight-leaf population after examining 152 anchors.
Archive that count and script. Those particular assignments are feasibility
only. Use a fresh seed 914202611 for the frozen 64-triplet development screen,
914202612 for confirmation, and 914202613 for power. Do not select seeds by scores.
The larger development sample improves precision while the headroom bound stays
unchanged. Freeze actual assignments and source before encoding.

## Headroom and representations

Keep all nine v1 baselines and their exact distance definitions, all three pinned
encoders (syntax, MiniLM, Lean-trained ReProver), four uniformly selected distinct
proofs and two intermediate positions (1,3), fixed fair tie bits, and the exact
one-sided 95% binomial upper bound <.90 for EVERY baseline. Structural, premise
and bag controls must tie by construction; inspect equality before inference.
Report half-credit scores as well as the randomized binary scores and both
premise-overlap strata. No cloud-distance scoring if this gate fails.

Reuse v1 tokenization, state renderer, Lean audit, pooling and encoder weights.
Verify a fixed two-triplet/24-proof fixture before encoding. Record all raw Lean
responses and compare every intermediate goal's AST with the symbolic state.
Checkpoint state vectors atomically during encoding so a reboot loses at most
one small batch. Resume only identical frozen plans, encoders and source files.

## Power before confirmation

Retain the primary criterion: ReProver energy must beat EACH of the nine cheap
controls by >=.10 observed accuracy and one-sided exact McNemar p<=.05. This
intersection-union rule is one primary claim, not selection of the easiest
baseline. Simulate 10,000 trials at N=256,512,1024 before viewing v2 scores.
Retain v1's strong-control planning alternative (.80 cloud, .65 each baseline,
cloud-only .25, baseline-only .10; conditional baseline independence). This is
conservative for the three matched/tied controls and is not fitted power for
the actual representations. Archive every joint result and paired comparison.

Evaluate both the all-controls-null (.65 each, discordance .35) and a composite
null with one equally accurate control and eight .50 controls, sharing the cloud
outcome. For the latter the equal control uses the same joint probabilities as
the all-null; other controls are independent fair bits. Select the smallest N
with alternative Wilson lower 95% bound >.80 and both null upper bounds <.10.
If none qualifies, stop before confirmation. Do not change the effect, criterion
or cap after outcomes. Report the exact headroom screen's operating characteristics
at N=64 separately; that calculation does not demonstrate a cloud effect.

## Conditional geometry check and confirmation

If headroom and power pass, compute energy on the frozen development clouds
for all three encoders, reporting all outcomes descriptively. Also run the fixed
100 proof-resampling stability draws (seed 914202614), each with four distinct
programs and the same positions 1,3. Recompute centroids for the SAME draws;
statement and symbolic controls stay fixed. ReProver energy must beat every
control by >=.10 in at least 80 draws. This is a conditional feasibility gate,
not a significance test, an independent replication, or a general sampling-power
envelope. Enumerating/encoding its additional possible states is allowed only
after the headroom screen; no replacement pairs or metric tuning.

If this fails, there is now a direct test of unordered energy's incremental
prediction on a screened population, but no confirmatory claim. Preserve it and
stop this operational candidate; do not substitute another distance after seeing
the result. If it passes, generate and freeze the power-selected nine-leaf
confirmation assignments using exactly the same matching/exclusion rules.
Use exhaustive shortest programs for each selected endpoint pair; an on-demand
graph enumeration may avoid materializing unused pairs but must agree with the
exhaustive small-graph reference. Insufficient disjoint eligible capacity closes
confirmation rather than weakening matching.

Only then acquire and Lean-verify every selected confirmation proof, check all
actual states, encode, and apply the exact primary rule. Publish all component
p-values, per-triplet outcomes, 10,000 triplet-bootstrap gain intervals and
secondary syntax/MiniLM energy results. Positive findings are restricted to
this synthetic rewrite relation, these encoders and this sampling budget.
No broad mathematical-semantic or cross-domain claim follows automatically.

The run ends at its first substantive failed gate or after the conditional
confirmation, with a complete report, code, reproducible vectors and checksums.
Further population/metric redesign requires a separately disclosed experiment.
