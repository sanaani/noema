# Confirmation without a minimum effect margin

## User-directed amendment and timing

After seeing the eight-leaf development result, the user explicitly rejected
the ten-percentage-point continuation requirement as arbitrary and instructed
continued work. This amendment removes that requirement and the associated
80-of-100 permission gate. The completed development study, original protocol,
all outcomes and failed original gate remain archived unchanged.

This is a new local Git preregistration before any nine-leaf assignments,
embeddings or scores. Development cannot supply confirmatory p-values for the
amended rule. No old candidate is searched for favorable pairs or rescored with
a replacement metric.

## Power, inference and uncertainty

The primary claim is positive added information over **every** cheap control.
For each of the nine paired comparisons, apply one-sided exact McNemar at .05
to ReProver energy versus that control. The claim passes only if all nine tests
pass. There is **no minimum accuracy-gain requirement**. This intersection-union
rule tests the single claim without selecting the most favorable baseline.
Report each component's accuracy, gain, discordant counts and exact p-value.

Report 10,000 triplet-bootstrap percentile 95% intervals for each gain (seed
914202617), clearly labeled marginal intervals. Also report descriptive scores
for syntax/MiniLM energy, premise-overlap strata, and tie-adjusted accuracies.
Do not treat secondary comparisons as alternative routes to primary success.

Before confirmation scores, calculate exact paired-test power at N=512,1024,
2048 for gains 0,.01,.012,.02,.03,.05 and total discordance .05,.10,.20,.35.
Sum over the binomial discordant count and its conditional exact rejection
probability; cases with gain greater than discordance are invalid. Archive the
calculations. The development centroid comparison has mean discordance .049375
and gain .011875, but these conditional summaries are planning information only.
The original .80-versus-.65 power claim does not carry over to such small gains.
N stays fixed at 512 regardless of this power table; report limited sensitivity
honestly rather than introduce another minimum-effect continuation gate.

No outcome-dependent sample-size changes or early significance stopping. Finish
all 512 triplets if acquisition succeeds, then publish the full effect estimates,
uncertainty, source/data archives and reproducibility checks. A nonsignificant
result does not establish absence of a small effect. Any positive result is
restricted to this synthetic transfer relation, encoder and sampling budget.
Broader mathematical domains and Phase 5 require evidence beyond this test.
