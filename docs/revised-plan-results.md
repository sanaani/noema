user identified the saturated-baseline task-design failure. The subsequent

There was enough encouragement in the original full-text association to proceed.
The new study qualifies a proof-cluster sampling budget, fixes a replay-granularity
confound, verifies a fresh corpus, matches proof lengths and state depths, and

The preregistered text cloud-minus-centroid requirement was at least .05 with
a paired 95% lower bound above zero. The observed gain is exactly zero.
Representation collapse does not cause this failure. The simpler baselines
already solve the task. Statement self-matching has a ceiling advantage, so
this task cannot establish that clouds have no additional information about
cross-theorem mathematical relationships. It establishes that such an advantage
was **not demonstrated here** and does not satisfy the frozen advancement rule.

Stricter premise-set diversity leaves capacity 16 per generator across the
required theorem set; tactic-histogram diversity leaves zero. Both are below
m=32. These sensitivities are recorded as unavailable, not run underpowered
or repaired by lowering the threshold. The less restrictive proof-identity and
state-sequence rule passes, and alternative disjoint proof splits reproduce.

All theorems belong to one generated Horn fragment with a common vocabulary
and conclusion. Initial contexts entail all eight atoms, so they can be
extensionally equivalent despite different premise-network presentations.
The truth encoder exposes goal-distribution structure; full syntax/text also
retain distinctive premise contexts. Reproducibility here concerns sampled
proof populations and their presentations. It is not evidence for a unique
geometry of logically equivalent propositions or for cross-domain motifs.

The neural encoder is pinned general-text MiniLM, not a validated mathematical
state model. The [training manifest](../results/formal-v1/training-manifest.json)
records the known training provenance and limits of contamination auditing.
Theorem-label permutations assume exchangeability of the designated alignment
under the conditional null. Bootstrap intervals resample both theorem axes
jointly and are conditional on the observed proofs; they are not simultaneous
guarantees over all proof populations or mathematics.

## Kill criteria and closure

Criteria 1 and 2 do not trigger as blanket reproducibility failures in this
canonical-replay population. Criterion 9 does not trigger: association survives
length/depth matching. Criterion 7 passes only for the selected restricted energy
regime. Criterion 8 fails for the native-replay design, then passes for the one
preregistered canonical redesign at the primary diversity rule; stricter diversity
coverage remains inadequate.

The decisive stopping condition is **criterion 4: no demonstrated added
information over simpler representations** in the specified task. Premise and
syntax explanations remain sufficient for the strongest full-content scores.
Cross-training robustness is not established. Criterion 5 is **untested**, not
a failed overlap p-value: no confirmatory pair universe was instantiated after
the upstream gate failed. H3/H4 across mathematical domains remain untested.

The conditional Phase 5 requirements remain explicit: a new held-out,
adequately sampled mathematical population, nongeometric eligibility thresholds
frozen before scores, every unordered eligible pair tested, and FDR over the
entire universe. No retrospective shortlist from this already examined
population can substitute for that design. The current research plan closes
with the bounded result above rather than a manufactured discovery.
